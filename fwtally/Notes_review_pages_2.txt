Classification Of Account 
1-Personal Account 
2-Real Account 
3-Nominal Account 
 
1-Personal Account:- 
 Those accounts that are created in the name of a person or an institution are called Personal Accounts (individual accounts), such as: Mohit, Rahul, Bank, Abc Company, Capital, Drawing 
etc. 
 
2-Real Account:- 
 Those accounts related to assets, property, or things are called Real Accounts, such as: Cash, Computer, Machinery, Furniture, Assets etc. 
 
3-Nominal Account:- 
 Those accounts related to profit-loss, income-expense, and purchase-sales are called Nominal Accounts, such as: Interest, Discount, Wages, Purchases, 
Sales, Profit & Loss etc.
