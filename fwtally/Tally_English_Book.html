and inventory management software that provides various facilities like Government

supported formats, multilingual operations, processing for small and medium

businesses in India. Tally products are transforming businesses across industry in over

Classification Of Account

1-Personal Account

2-Real Account

3-Nominal Account

1-Personal Account:-

Those accounts that are created in the name of a person or an institution are called Personal

Accounts (individual accounts), such as: Mohit, Rahul, Bank, Abc Company, Capital, Drawing

etc.

2-Real Account:-

Those accounts related to assets, property, or things are called Real

Accounts, such as: Cash, Computer, Machinery, Furniture, Assets etc.

3-Nominal Account:-

Those accounts related to profit-loss, income-expense, and purchase-sales are called Nominal Accounts, such as: Interest, Discount, Wages, Purchases,

Sales, Profit & Loss etc.

  • 1- Capital Account:-

All capital-related accounts are recorded under Capital Account. Example: if Ram starts business with 10,000, Ram is treated as Capital A/c.

  • 2- Loans Liabilities:-

Loans taken by the business are obligations of the business and are recorded under Loans Liabilities. Example: loan borrowed from Rahul against security.

  • 3- Current Liabilities:-

Short-term obligations are recorded under Current Liabilities, such as outstanding loans and creditors. Example: computer purchased on credit from Rahul.

  • 4- Fixed Assets:-

Long-term assets are recorded under Fixed Assets, such as building, machinery, plant, and equipment. Example: cell phone purchased for business use.

  • 5- Investment Account:-

Investment-related accounts are recorded under Investment Account, such as dividend investment, securities, or share investments.

  • 6- Current Assets:-

Short-term assets are recorded under Current Assets, such as cash account, bank account, debtors account, and advance account.

  • 7- Miscellaneous Exp. Assets:-

Deferred or preliminary expenses that are carried initially are recorded under Miscellaneous Exp. Assets.

  • 8- Suspense Account:-

If trial balance does not match, the temporary balancing difference is posted to Suspense Account.

  • 9- Branch Division:-

Accounts of branch offices created by the main company are recorded under Branch Division.

  • 10- Sales Account:-

All sales of goods are recorded under Sales Account, whether cash sales or credit sales.

  • 11- Purchase Account:-

All purchases of goods are recorded under Purchase Account, whether cash purchases or credit purchases.

  • 12- Direct Income:-

Income earned directly from business operations is recorded under Direct Income.

  • 13- Direct Expenses:-

Expenses directly related to business operations are recorded under Direct Expenses.

  • 14- Indirect Income:-

Income earned directly from business operations is recorded under Direct Income.

  • 15- Indirect Expenses:-

Expenses directly related to business operations are recorded under Direct Expenses.

  • 16- Reserve & Surplus:-

Reserves created from profit are recorded under Reserve & Surplus, such as capital reserve and redemption reserve.

  • 17- Bank Over Draft:-

When withdrawal exceeds bank balance, it is treated as Bank Overdraft (effectively a short-term borrowing from bank).

  • 18- Secured Loans:-

Loans backed by security are recorded under Secured Loans.

  • 19- Unsecured Loans:-

Loans backed by security are recorded under Secured Loans.

  • 20- Duties & Tax:-

All tax-related accounts are recorded under Duties & Tax.

  • 21- Provision:-

All provision accounts are recorded under Provision, such as provision for salary and provision for rent.

  • 22- Sundry Creditors:-

Parties to whom money is payable in future are recorded under Sundry Creditors.

  • 22- Sundry Debtors:-

Parties from whom money is receivable in future are recorded under Sundry Debtors.

  • 24- Deposit Assets:-

Money deposited as security for an asset or agreement is recorded under Deposit Assets.

  • 25- Loans & Advance Assets :-

Advances given and loan-like receivables are recorded under Loans & Advance Assets.

  • 26- Cash in hand:-

Cash balance maintained by the business is recorded under Cash in Hand.

  • 27- Stock on hand:-

Closing/available inventory is recorded under Stock on Hand.

  • 28- Bank Account:-

All bank ledgers are recorded under Bank Account, such as ICICI Bank, State Bank, and HDFC Bank.

  • 1- Cons Voucher:-

Contra Voucher is used for transactions between Cash and Bank, such as cash deposited into bank or cash withdrawn from bank.

2-Payment Voucher:-

Payment Voucher is used when payment is made to a person by cash or cheque.

  • 3- Receipt Voucher:-

Receipt Voucher is used when money is received, such as commission, discount, or cash income.

  • 4- Journal Voucher:-

Journal Voucher is used for non-cash/adjustment and credit transactions, including provisions and transfer entries.

  • 5- Sales Voucher:-

Sales Voucher is used to record all sales transactions, whether cash sales or credit sales.

  • 6- Purchase Voucher:-

Purchase Voucher is used to record all purchase transactions, whether cash purchases or credit purchases.

  • 7- Credit Score Voucher:-

Credit Note Voucher is used to record sales return (goods returned by customer).

  • 8- Debit Note Voucher:-

Debit Note Voucher is used to record purchase return (goods returned to supplier).

  • 9- Memorandum Voucher:-

Memorandum Voucher is used for provisional or reminder entries that may be converted later.

  • 10- Reserve & Surplus:-

Reserves created from profit are recorded under Reserve & Surplus, such as capital reserve and redemption reserve.
